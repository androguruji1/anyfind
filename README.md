# AnyFind — Asset Pack Manager for Premiere Pro

AnyFind turns any folder on your disk into a browsable, searchable pack of
editing assets — transitions, sound FX, LUTs, overlays, MOGRTs — with hover
previews and one-click import straight onto your timeline.

**Get it:** AnyFind is a paid panel — **[buy it on Superprofile](https://superprofile.bio/vp/anyfind-plugin)**.
You'll get the installer and your license key by email (14-day free trial included).

## Features

- **Folder-based packs** — point AnyFind at a folder; the sidebar mirrors its
  structure exactly, with unlimited nesting
- **Hover previews** for video, audio and images, plus real thumbnails pulled
  from inside `.mogrt` files
- **One-click import** — places the asset at the playhead on the lowest free
  track; video and audio pick their tracks independently, and tracks are added
  when none has room
- **MOGRTs go straight onto the timeline**
- **Live folder sync** — add, delete or rename files in Finder/Explorer and the
  panel follows, no refresh needed
- **Fast with huge libraries** — background indexing and a virtual-scrolling
  grid keep tens of thousands of assets smooth
- **Library tools** — search, favorites, Most Used view, colour filter and a
  duplicate finder
- **Optional ffmpeg fallback** for codecs the panel can't preview natively

## Compatibility

- Adobe Premiere Pro 2021 – 2026
- macOS and Windows 10/11
